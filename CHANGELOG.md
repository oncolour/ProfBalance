# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-10)

- Own icon from the ProfBalance logo on the minimap button and in the addon list.
- Minimap button sits on the rim for large or rescaled minimaps and respects square minimap shapes.
- CurseForge link in the TOC.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-10)

First public release for World of Warcraft: Forever (Interface 16001).

- Pace per profession: skill versus what your level expects, with In sync / Slightly behind / Behind / Far behind / Ahead.
- Data-driven target for Mining and Herbalism: the skill needed to gather ~80% of the node spawns in zones for your level. Other professions use the 5 x level rule of thumb.
- Rank reminders: tells you when Journeyman / Expert / Artisan can be trained, or which level unlocks it.
- Zone panel: the gathering nodes in your current zone, coloured green (gives skill-ups), gray (too easy) or red (locked, requirement shown). Skinning is judged against the zone's level range.
- Zone suggestions when the current zone has nothing left to give.
- Level-up summary in chat for professions that lag (toggle with /profbalance announce).
- Minimap button (toggle, hover overview, drag to move) and addon compartment entry.
- Slash commands: /profbalance, /pbal.
